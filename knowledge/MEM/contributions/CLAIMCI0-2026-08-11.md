### Business Summary

This service is the front door for a health-insurance claim. A caller asks it to
do one of three things with a single claim — **file a new claim, look it up, or
update it** — each identified by a unique claim ID. When a **new claim is filed**,
the service does not decide payment itself: it asks a **separate claims-rules
service** whether the claim can be auto-approved. If that service answers
"Accepted," the claim is marked **approved**; any other answer — or any failure to
reach the rules service — marks the claim **pended** (held for review). The
service recognizes three claim types — **drug, dental, and medical** — and treats
any unrecognized type as medical. Looking up or updating a claim that does not
exist returns "not found" rather than creating one. Every action and error is
written to an operations log. In short: it stores and retrieves claims and records
an **approve-or-pend** decision that is actually made by an external rules engine.
