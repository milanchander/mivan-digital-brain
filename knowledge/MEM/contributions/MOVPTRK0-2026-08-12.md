### Business Summary

The Overpayment Tracking System is the commercial claims recovery engine. It runs
nightly and takes in two streams: claims the adjudication system flagged as
possible overpayments (some held before payment, some already paid), and
overpayments found later by post-payment audit. For each, it decides whether real
money is recoverable, how much, and how to get it back — by **offsetting** it
against the provider's future payments, sending a **demand letter**, **writing it
off** if it is too small to pursue, or **referring to fraud investigators**. Very
large recoveries are held for human approval; very old cases are escalated. For
claims still in the adjudication loop it sends a decision back — release, adjust
to the correct amount, deny, or recover-and-re-adjudicate. Government-line
overpayments are handled by a different system under different rules. In short:
it turns "this claim was overpaid" into a tracked case and the right recovery
action, and hands loop claims back to adjudication.
